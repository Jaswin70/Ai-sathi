Privacy policy

This is a general draft based on how the website currently works. Update it if you add anything new (such as logins or online payments).

1. What we receive

  • The text you enter: when you run a tool, your text goes through our server to Google Gemini (an AI service) so it can generate the result or voice. Google's own terms and policies apply. On free AI services the provider may use inputs to improve its products, so never enter Aadhaar, PAN, bank/card details, passwords or any confidential personal information.
  • Image descriptions: to create an image, your description is sent to Google Gemini (to write and check an English prompt) and the resulting prompt is sent to Cloudflare (the image generation service). A photo you upload as a video background stays on your own device and is not sent to our server.
  • IP address: used temporarily to count daily limits and prevent abuse. Our hosting provider (Netlify) may also keep its own logs.
  • Pro code: saved in your browser so you don't have to enter it every time.

2. Videos and audio

Videos are made on your own device and are not stored on our server. For HD voice, only the text is sent to the server.

3. Payments

Payments are made directly by UPI. We never ask for your bank details or UPI PIN. The payment screenshot you send on WhatsApp is used only to verify your payment.

4. What we don't do

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with anyone except as needed to run the service or as required by law.

5. Children

This service is not intended for children to use on their own.

6. Contact

For questions or to ask us to delete your data, contact us on the WhatsApp number shown on the website.
